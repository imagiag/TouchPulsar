# Credits

**TouchPulsar** is pulsar synthesis for the Synthux Academy **Touch 2** (Daisy Seed).

## Research
- **Curtis Roads**, *Sound Composition with Pulsars*, Journal of the Audio Engineering Society 49(3), 2001 — the technique this instrument implements.

## Hardware and code from others
- **[Synthux Academy](https://www.synthux.academy/)** — the [Touch 2](https://www.synthux.academy/store/simple-touch-2) hardware. The pad, knob and switch drivers in `daisy/touch/` and the board photo `daisy/touch.jpeg` come from Synthux Academy's [TouchString](https://github.com/Synthux-Academy/TouchString) (DaisySP dependency replaced by a local clamp). See also [simple-touch-instruments](https://github.com/Synthux-Academy/simple-touch-instruments) (MIT).
  - TouchString contributors: [Vincent Maurer](https://github.com/vincent-maurer), [Rosa Schuurmans](https://github.com/vitrinekast), [Vlad Litvinenko](https://github.com/bleeptools).
- **[libDaisy](https://github.com/electro-smith/libDaisy)** by Electrosmith (MIT), used as a submodule via [Synthux's fork](https://github.com/Synthux-Academy/libDaisy).

## Development
Designed and played by the TouchPulsar author; engine, firmware, browser prototype and documentation written with Claude Code (Anthropic).
